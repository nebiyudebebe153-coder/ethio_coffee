OUR STORY

Coffee runs in our family

For generations, our family has farmed coffee in the highlands of Sidama — a region renowned the world over for its vibrant, fruit-forward coffees. What started as a small family farm is now a direct-export operation connecting our community's harvest with roasters across the globe.

We control every step — from the cherry on the tree to the green bean in the bag. No middlemen, no blending. Just pure, traceable Sidama coffee, grown with pride and shipped with care.

UNCOMPROMISING STANDARDS

Our Quality Commitment

Every lot that leaves our Sidama washing and drying stations undergoes rigorous testing to meet international specialty coffee standards.

SCA Grading & Cupping

Evaluated by certified Q-graders for sensory profiles, acidities, and clean finishes targeting SCA scores of 84+ to 88+.

Moisture Control

Strictly dried on raised bamboo beds until green beans reach an optimal 10.5%–11.5% moisture level for long storage life.

Defect Sorting

Triple-sorted by hand and machine to ensure Grade 1 and Grade 2 purity with minimal screen defect counts.

SEAMLESS LOGISTICS

Export Procedures & Shipping

We handle the end-to-end export workflow, ensuring full compliance with Ethiopian Coffee and Tea Authority protocols and international customs.

1

Contract & Sample Approval

Pre-shipment samples (PSS) are dispatched to buyers via express courier for cupping approval prior to final bagging.

2

Milling & Protective Packaging

Beans are hulled and packed in GrainPro hermetic bags inside standard 60kg or 69kg jute bags to preserve freshness and aroma during sea transit.

3

Inspection & Certification

Complete documentation is issued: Phytosanitary certificates, Certificates of Origin, SCA Quality Certificates, and Bill of Lading.

4

Port Logistics (Djibouti)

Direct inland transport from Sidama to the Port of Djibouti, loaded into 20ft/40ft ocean containers heading to destination ports worldwide.
