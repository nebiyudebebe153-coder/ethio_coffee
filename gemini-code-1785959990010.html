OUR STORY

Coffee runs in our family

For generations, our family has farmed coffee in the highlands of Sidama — a region renowned the world over for its vibrant, fruit-forward coffees. What started as a small family farm is now a direct-export operation connecting our community's harvest with roasters across the globe.

We control every step — from the cherry on the tree to the green bean in the bag. No middlemen, no blending. Just pure, traceable Sidama coffee, grown with pride and shipped with care.

Every step, in our hands

We manage the entire journey from cherry to shipment, so every bag arrives exactly as promised — fresh, graded, and traceable to its lot.

01

Harvest

Cherries are hand-picked at peak ripeness by our farming families across Sidama's highlands.

02

Process

Natural, washed, or honey — each lot is processed on raised beds and carefully turned by hand.

03

Every lot is screen-graded, defect-sorted, and cup-tested by our Q-graders before approval.

04

Export

We handle documentation, shipping, and logistics to deliver green coffee to your roastery door.
